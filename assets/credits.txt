Queryable Gaussian View Index — demonstration assets

Authors: Tianyu Xiong, Rui Li, Jiaqi Yang
Paper and project illustrations: QGVI authors, 2026.

The demo uses selected, cached research observations from nine scenes:
MatrixCity (Block A, Block Small), UC-GS (NYC, SF), and the HorizonGS
scene collection (CitySample, Colosseum, ElvenRuin, Park, Road).
The current paper cites these datasets and HorizonGS and describes
reconstruction and evaluation protocols.

Caption search: 78 selected posed views (41 original training images and
37 Gaussian grid renders), with stored captions. Full image frames are
preserved in proportional thumbnails. Some cached renders contain artifacts.
Reference-image and dataset rights remain with their respective creators.
This website does not assign a new license to the underlying scene assets.

RGB replay: saved LoGS geometric initialization and distinct-keypoint
acceptance decisions. The examples are synthetic-scene imagery.
Agent replay: the complete eight-decision block_a_002_q2 trace; images
are cached observations. Predicate checks and final review are model
assessments, not human ground truth. The example is selected for illustration.

Images used here do not require a Gaussian checkpoint or GPU to display.
No model calls, analytics, external fonts, or image uploads are made by the demo.
See the accompanying paper for the full methods, limitations, and references.
